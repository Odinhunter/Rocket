2dFixes — ranked levers, then changes wired to problems
THE FIX(ES) (highest-leverage first):
1Rewrite the hook to state the upgrade reason AND the price-per-serving vs their current Performance Whey on-surface — this resolves the two biggest consideration leaks at once.
2Convert the Informed Protein seal from a passive flicker into an active spoken claim fused to the isolate spec, so the ad builds new memory and a reason-to-buy instead of borrowing prior brand recall.
3Concentrate and sequence spend on weekend-browse and pre-purchase-research windows with a restock-timed retarget of save/linger audiences.
4Do NOT chase the lapsed, clean-label or snacker segments — their rejection is a structural trust/identity scar no in-scope creative lever can move.

Rebuild the headline around an explicit upgrade-and-cost argument: lead with the switch trigger ('Isolate, 27g vs the 25g in your Performance Whey — same Informed seal, ~₹X per serving') so the spec delta and the price-per-serving both live ON the creative surface.
